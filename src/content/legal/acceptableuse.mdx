Headpat is a platform for work made by people. **Uploading AI-generated art is not allowed.**

You may not post, upload, or otherwise make available through the Services any Contribution that:

- was generated, in whole or in substantial part, by a generative AI model or service, including but not limited to image, video, audio, and text generators;

This does not prohibit ordinary digital tooling that assists work you made yourself, such as brush stabilisation, colour correction, noise reduction, or selection and masking aids. The test is whether the creative expression in the Contribution originated with you.
