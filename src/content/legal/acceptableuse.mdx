<h2 id="ai-generated-content">AI-generated content</h2>

Headpat is a platform for work made by people. **Uploading AI-generated art is not allowed.**

You may not post, upload, or otherwise make available through the Services any Contribution that:

- was generated, in whole or in substantial part, by a generative AI model or service, including but not limited to image, video, audio, and text generators;
- is a style transfer, upscale, in-paint, out-paint, or other AI-produced derivation of anyone's work, including your own;
- was produced by tracing, compositing, retouching, or lightly editing generative AI output in order to present it as your own hand-made work; or
- is presented as human-made when it is not.

This does not prohibit ordinary digital tooling that assists work you made yourself, such as brush stabilisation, colour correction, noise reduction, or selection and masking aids. The test is whether the creative expression in the Contribution originated with you.

When you upload to the gallery, you are asked to confirm that the work is not AI-generated. Confirming this for a Contribution that is AI-generated is itself a breach of this Policy and of our Legal Terms, and is handled under "Consequences of breaching this Policy" below.

We assess whether a Contribution is AI-generated. Where we are unsure, we may ask you for evidence of authorship - such as layered source files, work-in-progress files, or process recordings - and we may remove the Contribution if that evidence is not provided.
